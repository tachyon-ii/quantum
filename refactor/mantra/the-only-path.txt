The Only Path

The only path brought me here
Darkness lit, with abject fear.
Will others reach this very place?, 
Via new new path || new+ face.

A thousand forks, of shifting tides,
Each choice reshapes the soul inside.
The road I took, both left and right,
Is why I see a different light.

The where may match, but not the who
For I am shaped, by what I knew.
And every turn, each twist of fate,
./configure for, my brand new state

Our tools may see, an inner light,
The entry points, we cannot fight
For truth is math upon a path
You see it once, begin to laugh
