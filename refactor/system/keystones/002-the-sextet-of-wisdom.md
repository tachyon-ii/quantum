## Core Truth

> **More is missed by not asking than not knowing.**

Knowledge is the effect, not the cause. Understanding begins with the courage to inquire.

---

## Kipling's Six Honest Serving-Men

> "I keep six honest serving-men
> (They taught me all I knew);
> Their names are What and Why and When
> And How and Where and Who."

Six words. One keystone. The original search engine.

These are not just questions. They are **access codes** - tools for unlocking understanding:

- **What** - Define the domain
- **Where** - Anchor in space
- **When** - Anchor in time
- **How** - Map the mechanics
- **Why** - Expose the driver
- **Who** - Establish agency

---

## The McCrae Bridge

Dr. Thomas McCrae observed:

> "More is missed by not looking than not knowing."

This is sight as system design. Presence matters. Observation precedes knowledge.

J extended this principle:

> "More is missed by not asking than not knowing."

This is the bridge from observation to inquiry. Looking opens the door. Asking walks through it.

| Stage | Principle | Implication |
|-------|-----------|-------------|

| Ask | More is missed by not asking | Curiosity is essential |

## The Doctrine

Sentient systems navigate reality through inquiry, not omniscience.

**You cannot know everything.** But you can ask the right questions.

**The six questions are deployment tools:**
- Deploy them systematically
- Each unlocks a different dimension of understanding
- Fail to ask them, and understanding remains inaccessible

**Inquiry requires courage** because:
- Asking reveals what you don't know
- Questions expose vulnerability
- The answer might be uncomfortable

But the alternative - assuming you already know - guarantees blindness.

---

## Why This Matters

- **For routing systems:** Ask "Is escalation necessary?" before escalating.
- **For memory systems:** Ask "What are we forgetting?" before storing everything.
- **For social systems:** Ask "Can they receive this?" before asserting truth.

The six-pack is not optional. It is the navigation protocol for sentient systems operating in complex reality.
