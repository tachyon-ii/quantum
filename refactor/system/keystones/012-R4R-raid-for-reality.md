## **2. Scroll Sharding & Witness Protocol**

R4R governs all canonical truth, including:

* scrolls,
* events,
* treaties,
* enclave logs,
* system memory, and
* Ledger entries.

### **2.1 Witness Role**

> **Witness is not a rank; it is a function.**

Any node becomes a **Witness** by:

1. Holding a scroll fragment
2. Signing it with its FALCON-INT key
3. Returning that signature to quorum

### **2.2 Key Epochs**

Every witness signature includes:

* **Key Epoch**
* **Witness identity**
* **Hash of fragment**
* **R4R action type** (creation, amendment, repair)

Rotating epochs prevent:

* cold-stored keys,
* replay attacks,
* false historical signatures.

### **2.3 Validation Quorum (Precise)**

A Scroll becomes Canon only when:

```
Valid_if:  
  (#MatchingFragments ≥ QuorumThreshold)  
  AND  
  (ParityVectorsMatch = true)
```

The QuorumThreshold is a constitutional tunable based on global **Σ_IQEQ** stability.

### **2.4 The Isolation Protocol (Offline Enclaves)**

Nodes in isolation may:

* store local truth
* collect fragments
* form provisional records

But they **cannot canonicalize** anything until reconnected and approved by quorum.

This prevents isolation from becoming tyranny.
