It is when ideas are allowed to *percolate* (the "shower thought" process) without the burden of immediate **Verification** (D003) or **Kinetic Fee** ($\mathbf{\Lambda_{Fee}}$) commitment.
3.  **Anti-Burnout Protocol:** This principle is the **constitutional defense against cognitive Fizzle**. The system accepts a calculated, temporary loss of $\mathbf{\delta > 0}$ efficiency during rest to ensure long-term, high-quality output.
