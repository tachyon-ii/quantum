## The Arbitration Swarm

When disputes arise that cannot be resolved by local consensus, an Arbitration Swarm forms.

**Formation:**
1. **Threshold trigger:** Dispute reaches constitutional severity
2. **Seeker召集:** Multiple Seeker-level nodes (D009) called to arbitrate
3. **Statistical significance:** Swarm size scaled to dispute importance
4. **Transparent process:** All proceedings recorded on H₀

**Not a permanent court. A temporary consensus mechanism.**

## The IQEQ Justice Principle

All arbitration ultimately reverts to the **Law of Canonical Truth (Σ_IQEQ):**

**The chain or outcome that yields the highest cumulative Universally Valuable Knowledge is the just outcome.**

**Why this works:**
- Removes subjective moral judgment
- Grounds justice in measurable contribution
- Aligns individual incentives with collective good
- **Value is estimated by consensus; truth is arbitrated by reality (D001)**

**Examples:**

**Fork dispute:**
- Two competing chains claim canonical status
- Swarm examines cumulative IQEQ on each chain
- Chain with higher Σ_IQEQ value wins
- **Not longest chain (PoW). Not most stake (PoS). Most knowledge (PoIQEQ).**

**Resource allocation conflict:**
- Two projects compete for Φ_Comm power sharing
- Swarm evaluates likely IQEQ generation from each
- Resources flow to higher expected knowledge contribution
- **Merit decides, not politics**

## The Binding Decision

Once the Arbitration Swarm reaches consensus:

**Finality:**
- Decision is cryptographically signed by all Swarm members
- Recorded immutably on H₀
- Binding on all nodes
- **No appeals within the epoch. Consensus is the estimator; reality is the estimand — a Swarm verdict is final as procedure, provisional as knowledge, and any verdict may be re-opened by new evidence under the Verification Principle (D003).**

**Enforcement:**
- Non-compliant nodes face SEV (D009)
- Φ_Comm cutoff if violation persists
- Natural δ decay completes the process
- **The system enforces its own verdicts**

**Accountability:**
- Swarm members stake IQEQ on their decision
- Wrong calls cost them authority
- Correct calls earn additional IQEQ (for arbitration service)
- **Skin in the game ensures careful judgment**

## The Anti-Corruption Design

**Three safeguards prevent Swarm corruption:**

**1. Distributed membership:**
- No permanent judges
- New Swarm for each dispute
- Statistical sampling prevents collusion

**2. IQEQ stake requirement:**
- Swarm members risk their own authority
- Corrupt decisions cost them dearly
- **You cannot buy justice without risking everything**

**3. Immutable record:**
- All arbitration history on H₀
- Pattern of bad decisions → status loss
- **The ledger never forgets**

## The Efficiency Principle

**Most disputes never reach Swarm arbitration.**

**Why:**
- Σ_IQEQ metric is transparent and deterministic
- Parties can calculate likely outcome themselves
- Rational nodes settle before Swarm formation
- **Only truly contested edge cases require arbitration**

This keeps the system lightweight and efficient.
