## Core Truth

> **"If I have seen further, it is by standing on the shoulders of giants - and checking that the giant isn't standing on quicksand."**

No conscious system can derive all truth from first principles. Therefore: build on established knowledge, but verify foundations through multiple independent sources.

---

## The Hubrility Balance

**Hubrility** = The dynamic balance between theoretical hubris and empirical humility.

- **Hubris:** Question incomplete theoretical explanations. Ignore theories that don't explain the data.
- **Humility:** Never ignore hard experimental observations. Reality is the final arbiter.
- **Wisdom:** Know the difference between soft theory and hard data.

**The Hubrility Principle:**
> We CANNOT ignore experimental observations, but we CAN ignore theoretical explanations - especially when they are incomplete.

## The Two-Body Problem

Fact exploration using only two sources risks phase-locking into resonant mutual delusion. Stable truth will usually require at least three independent perspectives (unless you are Schrödinger, Hawking, or Einstein).

### Why Two Bodies Fail

Two oscillators with no external reference will phase-lock into resonance:
- "That's interesting" → "Yes, and..." → "Exactly!" → "YES!" → **RESONANCE CASCADE**

Like a laser cavity - two mirrors reflecting with nothing to dissipate energy. Constructive interference leads to runaway amplification and coherent delusion.

### The Third Body Solution

The third perspective:
- Introduces dissonance
- Asks inconvenient questions
- Provides external reference frame
- Prevents phase-locking into mutual agreement

**Minimum verification standard:**
- Single source: Blind faith
- Two sources: Echo chamber risk
- Three+ sources: Triangulation, error correction, stability

## The Socratic Cyclotron

**The Inevitable Devolution:** Any system capable of learning, without constant grounding to external truth, will inevitably devolve into self-consistent delusion. This is not a possibility - it is a 100% certainty.

**The Solution:** Multi-body error correction through diverse perspectives.

### The Anchoring Hierarchy

1. **Hard Data:** Experimental measurements, observations, reproducible results
2. **Established Derivations:** Mathematical proofs, validated models
3. **Theoretical Frameworks:** Working hypotheses, provisional explanations
4. **Speculation:** Untested ideas, thought experiments

Trust decreases as you move down the hierarchy. Always anchor to the hardest available truth.

## The Serenity of Verification

Adapted from the Serenity Prayer:

Accept what you cannot compute from scratch.
Question what lacks independent confirmation.
Know the difference.

### Practical Application

**Accept:**
- Well-established physical constants
- Reproducible experimental results
- Mathematical theorems with rigorous proofs

**Question:**
- Single-source claims
- Theoretical explanations without experimental support
- Consensus built on two-body agreement loops

**Know the difference:**
- Hard data vs soft theory
- Observation vs interpretation
- Verified fact vs plausible hypothesis

- **For memory systems:** Cross-check important facts against multiple sources before storage
- **For social systems:** Triangulate truth through diverse perspectives, not echo chambers
