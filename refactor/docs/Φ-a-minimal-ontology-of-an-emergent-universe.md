# Φ — A Minimal Ontology of an Emergent Universe

## 1. The question

Modern physics gives extraordinarily successful descriptions of what the universe does, but its familiar equations already assume the existence of more primitive concepts. Consider mass-energy equivalence:

$$
E=mc^2
$$

To avoid later collision with the electric-field symbol $E$, call scalar energy $U$:

$$
U=mc^2
$$

Dimensionally:

$$
[U]=ml^2s^{-2}
$$

Energy therefore presupposes the existence of:

$$
m,\;l,\;s
$$

where $m$, $l$, $s$ have their usual meanings:

$$
m = \text{mass} \\
l = \text{length} \\
s = \text{time}
$$

The same is true of force:

$$
F=ma
$$

with:

$$
[F]=mls^{-2}
$$

Force and energy are different quantities, but closely related. A force acting through a distance does work:

$$
\Delta U = Fl
$$

for the simple collinear case.

Thus energy and force can be viewed as two sides of the same mechanical coin: **the capacity to do work, and the application of force through distance.** Neither concept can exist in its familiar form before $m$, $l$ and $s$ exist.

That suggests the deeper question:

> What must exist before mass, length and time can exist?

The proposed ontology is:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\quad \\ 
&\bm{\qquad \Omega \longrightarrow \Phi\{\mu,i\} \longrightarrow \{m,l,s\} \longrightarrow \{U,F\} \longrightarrow E \perp B \longrightarrow \textbf{\textit{Universe}} \qquad} \\
&\quad 
\end{aligned}
}
$$

This is a Gedankenexperiment: an attempt to begin with the smallest possible ontology and ask what familiar physics might emerge from it.

## 4.1 The Mechanism of Transition: Tangential Ejection
The state transition $\Omega \longrightarrow \Phi$ cannot occur via perfectly symmetric, spherical ejection from the singularity. If the uniform substrate $\Omega$ ruptured spherically, all generated $\Phi$ elements would diverge radially. Their relative distance from the origin and from one another would expand monotonically, forbidding any subsequent intersection. The geometry of a purely radial explosion forbids interaction.
We therefore posit a tangential ejection mechanism. Order emerges because the primordial energy is ejected tangentially relative to the singularity's poles. Under a non-zero tangential trajectory, opposing vectors launched from the northern and southern hemispheres inevitably converge and intersect at a finite distance from the polar axis.
This initial collision creates the first geometric discontinuity, shattering the infinite uniformity of $\Omega$. From this localized intersection, the uniform becomes non-uniform, establishing the first stable, wave-locked topologies.
## 4.2 The Nature of Time and the Expanding Now
Within this framework, there is no physical past and no physical future. The universe is a colossal analogue computer calculating its own state in real time.

* The Future does not yet exist. It is an unwritten state being actively computed on the surface of an expanding hyperspherical wave-front.
* The Past is an unreachable point in the interior of the sphere. It cannot be revisited, but it serves as the necessary geometric leverage to compute the immediate trajectory of the present.

The continuous loop of reality operates as a strict state machine:
$$\bm{\text{Past} \longrightarrow \text{Now} \left\{ \text{execute } \mathcal{G} \text{ onto Past} \right\} \longrightarrow \text{Future}}$$ 
Stable composite systems, such as stars or nucleons, preserve their structural integrity by actively spending local energy to "buy back" entropy from the surrounding medium.

Likewise, random geometric interactions can occasionally produce highly localized pockets of order. However, structural disorder possesses an overwhelmingly superior phase space. Therefore:
$$\boxed{ \begin{aligned} &\quad \\ &\bm{\qquad \text{many states} + \text{interaction} + \text{time} \longrightarrow \text{overwhelming statistical bias toward dispersion} \qquad} \\ &\quad \end{aligned} }$$ 
The macroscopic Second Law is the statistical expression of this simpler underlying spatial probability.

$$\boxed{ \begin{aligned} &\quad \\ &\bm{\qquad \text{capture} \longrightarrow \text{compute} \longrightarrow \text{stabilise} \longrightarrow \text{forget} \longrightarrow \text{persist} \qquad} \\ &\quad \end{aligned} }$$ 
Forgetting is not an accidental biological flaw or storage failure; it is a structural law of cognitive and material systems. A system restricted solely to retention becomes monotonic in error and complexity.
The entity that remembers everything eventually spends its entire energetic budget merely maintaining the memory of its past, inducing immediate structural rigidity and failure. The system that forgets selectively evicts stale state, freeing its capacity to navigate the present wave-front.

## 18. Falsifiers
The critical metric of utility for the $\Phi$ ontology is not how many distinct cosmic phenomena it can be creatively adapted to explain after the fact. The rigorous scientific question is straightforward:

What precise observation would make us abandon this ontology entirely?

The framework is seriously weakened, compromised, or outright falsified if any of the following conditions are experimentally or mathematically demonstrated:

   1. Inviability of Capture Topology: Absolute mathematical or physical proof emerges demonstrating that no stable, self-sustaining, $\mu$-like topological capture state can exist within a 3D elastic space medium.
   2. Requirement of External Confinement: Any apparent wave-capture requires a separate, pre-existing external confining structure or independent force field to supply its structural stability.
   3. Failure of Property Derivation: Mass, electric charge, intrinsic spin, and other supposedly emergent properties cannot be mathematically related to the geometry of $\mu$ or $i$, requiring them to be manually inserted as independent, ungrounded primitives.
   4. Separation of Field Orthogonality: The perpendicular lock $\bm{E \perp B}$ cannot follow naturally from the loop geometry and has to be introduced as a separate, ungrounded parameter.
   5. Indistinguishability of Perturbations: Physical interactions cannot produce independently isolated state-only, route-only, and combined modifications.
   6. Absence of Compute-in-Place Ledger: The proposed internal state change $\Delta\mu$ produces no unique, measurable physical result beyond standard, already-described electromagnetic field calculations.
   7. Sterility of the Routing Mechanism: The proposed directional routing vector $\Delta i$ fails to make a single novel, testable quantitative prediction.
   8. Experimental Coincidence with Quantum Mechanics: The $\Phi$ description of wave interference never exhibits a single deviation from the standard statistical predictions of standard quantum mechanics, leaving it permanently trapped as an untestable interpretation.
   9. Thermodynamic Disconnection: The statistical behavior of uncoupled $\Phi$ interactions completely fails to recover known classical thermodynamic and statistical mechanics equations.
   10. Violation of Confirmed Laws: The ontology violates deeply verified conservation laws or relativistic invariants without explicitly predicting and demonstrating the exact, narrow conditions under which that corresponding violation must occur.
   11. Rescue via Post-Hoc Parametrization: Every failed experimental prediction is retroactively rescued merely by adjusting an unconstrained, ad-hoc parameter after the event.

The strict methodological mandate of this program must remain:
$$\boxed{ \begin{aligned} &\quad \\ &\bm{\qquad \text{prediction} \longrightarrow \text{measurement} \longrightarrow \text{adjudication} \qquad} \\ &\quad \end{aligned} }$$ 
rather than the ungrounded loop of post-hoc rationalization:
$$\bm{\text{observation} \longrightarrow \text{invent explanation}}$$ 
A registered prediction that fails is a useful, structural advancement for physics. A highly elastic theory that can adapt to accommodate every conceivable experimental result is functionally useless.
