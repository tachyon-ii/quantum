# Part I — Three axioms, before terminology

## 1. The question

Modern physics presupposes what it describes. $E = mc^2$ has dimensions $m\,l^2\,s^{-2}$; $F = ma$ has $m\,l\,s^{-2}$; neither can exist in its familiar form before mass, length and time exist. So:

> **What must exist before mass, length and time can exist?**

v1 answered: $\Omega \to \Phi\{\mu,i\} \to \{m,l,s\} \to \{E,F\} \to V\perp M \to$ Universe. v2 keeps the chain and corrects its head. Three things cannot be avoided and are stated as axioms; two things v1 posited turn out to be names for things already present.

## 2. The proto-locale, and what an axiom is

The number line is an axiom; primes are a human reading of it — interesting, useful (cryptography rests on them), and *ours*, not the line's. The same for binary: a GPU is "binary" because someone declared < 0.3 V to be 0 and > 0.7 V to be 1, with the band between undefined. The threshold is the axiom; the silicon doesn't know.

An axiom in a Gedankenexperiment is an indivisible posit. It is not true or false; it is fertile or sterile, and the probability it is correct is the probability of what it explains (keystone 001: *all models are wrong; reality is ground truth*). Inside Ω there is no reference frame, so no current-physics statement is admissible there — not as objection, and not as support. [RULE, James.]

## 3. Axiom Ω₁ — proto-amount  [AXIOM]

Ω has an *amount*: something that can be shed in portions and is conserved across the shedding. Ejection without a quantity is a word, not an event. In our later terminology the conserved amount is called energy, and the identity is bookkeeping across the emergence, not a derivation from it:

$$
\sum \Omega = \sum E
$$

[James, 10 Oct. The v1 justification "because $E = mc^2$ says so" is struck under the proto-locale rule; this one needs nothing downstream.] One consequence falls out immediately: any sector we cannot account for in $\sum E$ is a shortfall in our accounting, not in Ω. The dark sectors of §16 are ledger entries in $\sum\Omega$ before they are anything else.

## 4. Axiom Ω₂ — proto-spin, the 0th dimension  [AXIOM]

Ω has one further property and only one: spin. Two legs carry it, and both are needed.

**The leg that selects.** Spin is the *only* property a partless whole can carry. Charge needs a sign relative to something; colour needs others; position needs a where. A phase needs nothing — a single internal angle, zero metres, one degree of freedom. That is why it sits at dimension zero. [James: "spin is the 0th dimension"; the pre-spatial form is a $U(1)$ phase over a point.]

**The leg that makes it non-zero.** Of the continuum of values the phase rate could take, exactly one is zero. $P(\text{spin}=0) = 1/\infty \to 0$; spin is present with measure one. [James.] On its own this leg selects nothing — it gives measure one to *every* continuous property — which is why it must be paired with the first. Paired, the posit is: *the one property available is non-zero with probability one.* There is no better posit; there was a better statement of it.

**Mach, before the first ejection.** Rotation relative to what? Before anything has left Ω there is nothing outside it, so "spin" has no referent — which is exactly why it is *proto*-spin and belongs at dimension 0: a phase, not a rotation. The first ejecta turn phase into rotation by giving it something to be measured against. Newton's bucket, solved by ejecting the room. [James, 10 Oct; POSIT.]

**What is struck.** v1's "without spin the proto-universe suffers immediate gravitational collapse" imports gravity, which is downstream of $m$, and "spherical", "equator" and "radius", which are downstream of $l$. In Ω's own ordering that justifies the parent by its children. The admissible form (the ChatGPT bee's wording, adopted): *Ω admits intrinsic cyclicity; the non-cyclic branch stays undifferentiated.* [ASSERTED → adopted as the pre-metric statement.]
