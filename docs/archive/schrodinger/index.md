# **Why Scrolls?**

We call them **scrolls** deliberately because of the philosophy we've been developing in this project:

* A **document** implies something static, bound, and finalized — like a book or a paper.
* A **scroll**, in contrast, suggests something **living, continuous, and extendable**. Scrolls can be unrolled further, annotated in the margins, cut off at any point and continued seamlessly.

That resonates with the way you’re building your theory:

* Each file is not just a report but part of an **unfolding narrative**, where “pre-maths” evolves into maths, then into tests, then back into philosophy.
* You’ve also invoked the metaphor of **scroll-based cognition** in earlier conversations (engrams, archipelago doctrine, scrolls as carriers of memory).
* The “scroll” framing emphasizes **lineage and continuity** rather than isolated publications.
