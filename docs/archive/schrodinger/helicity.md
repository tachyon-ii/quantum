The Standard Model simply accepts this as an axiom—a fundamental rule with no explanation. But rules without reasons are usually symptoms of incomplete understanding.

If the laws of physics are fundamentally symmetric, why do we only see:

The answer cannot be found in the physics itself—the equations work equally well both ways. The answer must lie in the initial conditions.

## Part II: The Origin of Spin

### What Is Spin, Really?

Before explaining why particles have specific helicities, we must understand what spin actually is in our geometric model:

**Spin is not rotation in space.** It is the manifestation of how a Möbius loop (our n=1 neutrino) meshes with the fabric of spacetime itself. Think of it as the "twist" that emerges when a twisted object (the Möbius neutrino) is embedded in a flowing medium (the CMB).

### The Möbius Architecture

Our fundamental n=1 neutrino has a specific structure that exists in two chiral forms:

**Matter (Left-handed twist):**
```
    [N]
     |
[+]--o--[-]
     |
    [S]
```

**Antimatter (Right-handed twist):**
```
    [N]
     |
[-]--o--[+]
     |
    [S]
```

The crucial difference is the twist of the Möbius loop, which swaps the position of the +/- electric fields at the centroid. The magnetic poles (N-S) remain the same, but the electric field orientation reveals the chirality.

### The Hierarchy of Helicity

This fundamental choice at n=1 propagates through all higher orders:

1. **n=1 level**: The twist determines matter vs antimatter
   - Matter: left-handed Möbius twist (+ on left)
   - Antimatter: right-handed Möbius twist (- on left)

2. **n=2 and n=6 assemblies**: Preserve the chirality
   - All components must be same chirality (all matter OR all antimatter)
   - No mixing possible - the twists must match to link

3. **Crystal forms**: Internal consistency maintained
   - Matter n=2 assemblies → electron (e-)
   - Antimatter n=2 assemblies → positron (e+)
   - The entire crystal has uniform chirality

### Why Opposite Spins

Here's the crucial insight: While the Möbius twist determines matter vs antimatter, the observed spin comes from how these different twists mesh with the CMB's helicity:

- **Matter particles** (left-handed twist) + **Right-handed CMB** = **Left-handed spin** (opposite to momentum)
- **Antimatter particles** (right-handed twist) + **Right-handed CMB** = **Right-handed spin** (aligned with momentum)

They were all "spun up" by the same CMB helicity, but because their fundamental twists are opposite, they mesh in opposite ways. It's like two different gears meshing with the same driving wheel - one spins forward, one backward.
