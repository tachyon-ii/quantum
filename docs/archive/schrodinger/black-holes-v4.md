# Black Holes: A Hypothetical Model

## Introduction

The standard model of gravitational collapse predicts the formation of a singularity—a point of infinite density where known physics ceases to apply. A necessary consequence is that black holes should be uniform, static objects fully described by mass, charge, and spin (the "No-Hair Theorem"). This prediction stands in stark contradiction to observation.

## The Observed Diversity

The universe presents a vast diversity of black hole behaviors that cannot be explained by external parameters alone:

Quiescent systems: Sagittarius A* remains remarkably stable despite its enormous mass
Active systems: M87* and distant quasars launch powerful jets and exhibit complex variability
Transitional systems: Objects that switch between active and dormant states

This diversity exists even among black holes of similar mass and spin, suggesting their behavior depends on internal state, not just external parameters. The uniformity predicted by the singularity model is absent from nature.

## The Uniformity Problem

## Resolution Through Non-Singular Dynamics

This document proposes that black holes do not collapse to singularities but to finite-density, dynamic cores. In this picture, ultracompact, finite-density cores undergo pulse-jet cycles: trapped fusion energy (from pre-iron elements) accumulates until reaching a critical threshold, triggering partial degeneration/annihilation that releases a burst temporarily reducing the mass before the system recovers. Crucially, the model predicts prolonged stability and dormancy in the absence of accretion due to the endothermic nature of post-iron fusion acting as a natural coolant.

The model's central predictions include: (i) multi-scale ring structure with stable geometric ratios reflecting internal layering, (ii) fuel-dependent variability where pulse recurrence depends on accretion rate and internal composition, not mass alone, (iii) a two-timescale variability, where the long-term **recurrence** is a complex function of fuel and internal state, while the short-term **pulse duration** ($T_{pulse}$) may scale with gravitational time, (iv) predictable **correlations between spatial and temporal observables** driven by the internal engine, (v) a hard upper mass limit from thermal instability, and (vi) sub-percent transients during mergers. ## Part I: Core Propositions

### 1.1 The Non-Singular Black Hole

We consider non-singular black hole interiors—ultracompact, finite-density cores matched to an exterior Kerr/Schwarzschild solution. This interior is a **model assumption** designed to be consistent with exterior Kerr; we are not altering GR's local field equations. Locally regular orthonormal frames cross the horizon smoothly; globally, the event horizon remains a null causal boundary where escape velocity equals c.

### 1.2 Dynamical Horizon Permeability (Effective)

**Fundamental Postulate:** Perfect, eternal event horizons require exact stationarity. Real black holes are dynamical and asymmetric; their **trapping horizons** move, transiently lowering redshift barriers and enabling limited outward transport without violating local causality.

Sources of asymmetry include:
- Accretion streams and clumpy infall
- Rotation and frame-dragging effects
- Tidal forces from companions
- Internal dynamics and gradients

Non-axisymmetric dynamics create **transient low-redshift corridors** (macroscopic, GR-consistent), through which outward radiation can outrun a **retreating trapped surface**. Local c remains invariant. This principle:
- Enables the pulse-jet mechanism without violating causality
- Mitigates information-loss intuitions by allowing limited outward transport in non-stationary geometries (local unitarity claims are beyond scope)
- Explains effective magnetic field behavior at horizons
- Predicts correlation between asymmetry and activity levels

**Note:** "Permeability" denotes motion of **trapping horizons** and temporary lowering of redshift barriers in non-stationary, asymmetric geometries—**not** superluminal transport or literal holes in a global event horizon.¹

### 1.3 Density Distribution Within Black Holes

Black holes exhibit a density gradient:
- Maximum (but finite) density at the core
- Progressively decreasing density approaching the event horizon
- Continuous physics across the horizon boundary (no local divergence)

### 1.4 The Geometric Confinement Model

In this framework, matter at extreme densities consists of effective charged modes in geometric confinement, requiring sustained orbital motion. As compression reduces available volume, required trajectories become incompatible with stability beyond a critical density, generating a pressure-like back-reaction that opposes further collapse. ## Part II: Matter Under Extreme Compression

### 2.1 The External Fusion Furnace: The Accretion Disk

The primary, continuous fusion of light elements (H → He → ... → Fe) occurs in the superheated, high-pressure environment of the **accretion disk, outside the event horizon**. This external furnace is the source of an Active Galactic Nucleus's (AGN's) persistent luminosity.

The accretion disk acts as a self-sorting engine. Exothermic fusion releases energy that, via the Poynting-Robertson effect, removes angular momentum from the resulting "ash," efficiently driving it inward. The endothermic barrier at Iron-56 acts as a natural thermostat, limiting the production of super-heavy elements. Therefore, the material that primarily falls into the black hole is pre-processed **Iron-56 ash** and residual un-fused fuel.

### 2.2 The Compression Limit

The geometric confinement model provides natural resistance to infinite collapse:

**The Motion Requirement:** Effective charged modes in confinement require sustained orbital motion; as volume shrinks, required trajectories become incompatible with stability beyond a critical density, generating back-pressure that opposes further collapse.

**Electromagnetic Back-Pressure:** The repulsion between charged components provides outward pressure that increases with compression, creating a natural lower bound on volume.

### 2.3 Phase Transitions Under Extreme Conditions

At critical temperature/pressure, matter undergoes phase transitions:
1. Geometric confinement structures deform
2. Confinement weakens progressively
3. Matter degenerates toward fundamental components
4. Partial annihilation becomes possible

## Part III: The Pulse-Jet Mechanism

### 3.1 The Internal Energy Budget: A Dynamic Equilibrium

With the primary fusion furnace now external, the core's dynamics are governed by a battle between heating and cooling.
* **Heating Sources:** The core is constantly heated by (1) the immense **kinetic and thermal energy** of the infalling iron ash, and (2) ongoing **gravitational compression**.
* **Cooling Sink:** The core is constantly cooled by **endothermic fusion**, as the immense pressure forces the iron ash to fuse into heavier, trans-uranic elements. This "fusion refrigerator" consumes thermal energy to create mass.

An active black hole exists in a **dynamic equilibrium** where the heating from accretion is balanced by the cooling from endothermic fusion. The stability we observe is this equilibrium of powerful, opposing forces.

### 3.2 The Self-Regulating Cycle and Annihilation Trigger

The pulse-jet is a catastrophic event that occurs when this equilibrium is broken.

1.  **Accumulation Phase (Equilibrium):** The core accretes iron ash, and the heating/cooling processes remain in balance, resulting in a stable, "simmering" state.
2.  **Release Phase (The Trigger):** A large influx of new fuel allows the heating rate to overwhelm the cooling rate of the endothermic fusion. This pushes the core's temperature and density past a critical threshold, causing the **geometric confinement of the nucleons to fail.** This failure enables a massive **core annihilation** event.
3.  **The Pulse:** The instantaneous energy release from annihilation drives a powerful pressure wave, which is the engine of the pulse-jet.
4.  **Recovery Phase (Enforced Quiescence):** The pulse expels the volatile fuel. The core, now composed of stable high-Z material, is dominated by the cooling effect of endothermic fusion, enforcing a long period of dormancy until a new, significant accretion cycle begins.

**Energy Budget:** If a burst reduces mass by fraction f, the horizon radius drops by f, releasing energy E ~ fMc² (observed externally modulo redshift and beaming). For M ~ 10⁹ M☉, f = 10⁻⁴ implies E ~ 10⁵⁹ erg.

### 3.3 The Entropy Processing Function

Black holes serve (in this model) as the universe's constitutional entropy processors - preventing permanent concentration of ordered structures:

**Process:**
- Consume complex bound structures at any scale
- Break down via extreme compression and temperature
- Output maximum-entropy radiation via pulses and eventual evaporation
- Ensure no structure becomes permanently concentrated

## Part IV: The Permeable Horizon - Physical Mechanism

### 4.1 How Asymmetry Creates Permeability

The permeable horizon principle (Section 1.2) enables energy escape through asymmetry-induced channels. These channels form through:

**Gradient Formation:**
- Different regions near the horizon have varying effective refractive indices (n_g)
- Sharp boundaries between regions create discontinuities
- Energy can transfer across these boundaries through refraction and mode conversion

**Channel Dynamics:**
- Channels open and close on dynamical timescales
- Size distribution from nucleon-scale to macroscopic
- Larger asymmetries create more and bigger channels

### 4.2 The Pulse-Jet Exploitation Mechanism

The internal pulse mechanism exploits pre-existing permeability:

1. **The Trigger:** Internal phase transition from matter-dominated to radiation-dominated state generates immense outward pressure
2. **The Channels:** Asymmetries have already created escape routes
3. **The Escape:** Pressure wave forces material through these transient channels
4. **The Result:** Mass-energy escapes, horizon adjusts to new total mass

The pressure wave doesn't create exit routes; it provides motive force through routes intrinsic to any real black hole.

### 4.3 Quantitative Considerations

The permeability rate depends on:
- **Rotation parameter:** a* = J/(Mc) 
- **Accretion rate:** Ṁ/Ṁ_Edd
- **Tidal strength:** From binary companions
- **Internal pressure:** From fusion and phase transitions

Order-of-magnitude estimate for escape fraction:
$f_{escape} \sim \epsilon \cdot (a_*^2 + \dot{M}/\dot{M}_{Edd})$
where ε ~ 10⁻⁶ - 10⁻⁴ depending on internal dynamics.

### 4.4 Observable Consequences

**Magnetic Field Threading:** Field lines exploit the same channels, enabling jet formation

**Activity Correlation:** More violent dynamics → more channels → higher activity

**Information Preservation:** No perfect trap means no information destruction

**Merger Enhancement:** Violent dynamics during mergers maximize channel formation

**The Central Prediction — Correlated Observables from an Inaccessible Engine:**

* **Spatial-Temporal:** Changes in the geometric **ring structure** should be a precursor to **flare events**, with a characteristic and predictable time lag.
* **Spectral-Polarimetric:** The **hardening of the emission spectrum** during a pulse should be correlated with a specific swing in the **electric vector position angle (EVPA)**.
* **Cross-Frequency Coherence:** The timing of peaks in different wavebands (e.g., X-ray vs. radio) should exhibit stable phase relationships, reflecting that they are driven by the same common engine.

**Additional Testable Predictions:**

2.  **Stable Geometric Ring Ratios:** The multi-ring structure should maintain consistent geometric ratios over long timescales, reflecting the stable layers of the interior, which impress their gravitational signature on the external plasma.
3.  **Dual-Timescale Variability:** Black hole activity exhibits two distinct timescales:
     * A complex, long-term **Flare Recurrence Time ($T_{recur}$)**, which depends on fuel rate, mass, and internal composition.
     * A short-term **Pulse Duration ($T_{pulse}$)**, the intrinsic profile of a single flare, which may scale with the black hole's mass.
4.  **Maximum Black Hole Mass:** Thermal instability sets upper limit M_max(α, Ṁ) ~ 10¹⁰ M☉

5. **Multi-Scale Ring Ratios:** Stable ratios between preferred scales (geometric sequence or mode-locking)

6. **Spectral-Polarimetric Signature:** Pulse releases show correlated hardening + EVPA swings

7. **Merger By-Products:** Sub-percent EM/neutrino bursts coincident with ringdown

8. **Strong-Field Corrections:** At b/r_s = 20, first-order deflection (4M/b) is ~7-8% low vs exact

### 5.2 Distinguishing Features

This model differs from standard GR predictions:
- Complex multi-scale structure rather than clean photon rings
- Periodic variability from internal dynamics with T ∝ t_g
- Hard upper mass limit from thermal instability
- Continuous local physics but global causal boundary preserved

### 5.3 Falsification Criteria

The model is falsified if:
- Multi-scale ring ratios vary significantly between observation epochs (indicating transient rather than structural origin)
- Pulse recurrence shows simple mass scaling independent of accretion history
- No correlation between internal composition proxies and activity levels
- Individual pulse durations show no gravitational time scaling

## Part VI: Open Questions and Challenges

### 6.1 Theoretical Challenges

- Computing exact threshold for geometric confinement failure
- Detailed modeling of radiation pressure wave propagation
- Reconciling pulse mechanism with observed quiescent black holes
- Predicting exact spectral signatures of heavy-element core

### 6.2 Observational Challenges and the Quiescence Solution

**The Dormancy Challenge:** Black holes like Sgr A* remain quiet for millions of years despite having this internal engine.

**The Solution - Fuel-Limited Stability:** The pulse-jet mechanism is fundamentally **fuel-limited**:
- After a pulse, light-element "detonator" fuel is expelled
- Remaining core is high-Z (post-iron) material where fusion is **endothermic**
- Endothermic fusion acts as a powerful coolant - a "fire extinguisher"
- System driven to **prolonged, stable quiescence**
- Engine cannot restart without significant new accretion of fusible material

This explains why dormant black holes are stable: they have exhausted their fuel supply and are cooled by endothermic processes, not because they lack an internal mechanism.

**Other Observational Challenges:**
- No definitively observed black hole "detonations"
- Growth history of SMBHs suggests stability across wide mass range
- Need higher resolution to test multi-scale predictions

### 6.3 Key Uncertainties

- Critical temperature/pressure for phase transition
- Timescales for complete pulse cycles
- Efficiency of energy escape during pulses
- Role of rotation and magnetic fields
