## 2. One solid, three readings  [GEOMETRIC IDENTITY]

The n = 2 governor — two loops entangled at a centroid — relaxes from the planar cross to the regular tetrahedron because four repelling lobes on a sphere are universally optimal there (Cohn–Kumar 2007; square 3.83 → tetrahedron 3.67, v2 §7). Stand it on the now-surface: **three lobes in the surface, one along the normal.** A tripod, with its fourth leg in time.

Regular tetrahedron, edge $a$: height $a\sqrt{2/3} = 0.816a$; centroid-to-vertex $R = a\sqrt6/4 = 0.612a$ for *all four* vertices; centroid a quarter of the way up, so the base plane sits $R/3$ behind it and the apex $R$ ahead; apex-to-base-vertex angle $\arccos(-1/3) = 109.47°$.

**Reading 1 — quantum space.** The three base lobes are the surface's quantum of extent: the figure's base. Orthogonal to time by construction (v2 §12a: t ⊥ space is the synchronous gauge).

**Reading 2 — quantum time.** The apex lobe is the next tick. It is the same distance $R$ from the centroid as each space lobe. *One quantum of time and one quantum of length are the same distance because the figure is regular:* $\ell = c\,t$ with **c = 1 as a symmetry of the solid**, not a convention.

**Reading 3 — gravity.** The apex is the lobe that has *left the plane* (the 2D → 3D snap, v2 §7, James 15 Oct). A surface with something pulled out of it is a stretched surface; the pull is along the normal, i.e. along time; and the base sits $R/3$ behind the centroid — **every capsid is one-third of a tick in the past** by construction. That is v2 §12a's dent and lag — *mass is behind the chorus* — with a per-capsid number, additive over capsids, which is Newton's superposition.

So the tripod gives: space ⊥ time; c = 1; the lag; and linearity. Four results from one figure.

## 3. The origin of G  [mechanism complete; value owed]

Two ledgers, kept apart because the first draft mixed them.

**The size ledger — compression and tension.** Extent is period (posit 1). A capsid spends part of it circulating in space — the lobes run at c — and has less left for the advance. **Mass is behind the chorus because c is a budget, not a limit.** This is reading 3 of §2 as kinematics, and it is axiom four derived. ### 3a. The horizon is not where the capsid deforms  [COMPUTED; equivalence principle]

### 5a. Why the turtle doesn't soften  [James's objection, 15 Oct; answered]

If G were an *extensive* property of the surface — a total elastic energy spread over a growing area — it would fall as the universe expands, and that is variant gravity, dead at $\dot G/G < 10^{-13}$/yr. The answer is that the stiffness is a **tension** — energy per unit area, intensive — which is what a soap film has: the film does not get floppier as you blow the bubble; its *curvature* falls, $H = 2\gamma/R$, while $\gamma$ is unchanged. Here G is $\gamma$ and the Hubble rate is $H$: the one that falls is the one that is measured to fall. The second half is **conformal lockstep**: the tick scales with extent (posit 1), so time expands with space and the "arc" of curvature per capsid is constant in capsid units. A fit without fitting — and §3 says why: the tension is the per-link rule, which has no knowledge of how many links there are.

## 6a. The onion  [James, 15 Oct evening; mapped to GR to the exponent]

## 8. Falsifiers

1. **γ = 1 by construction** (v2 §12a, 32.11a). The bending is all in space; any measured γ ≠ 1 kills reading 3.
2. **Linearity.** Gravity additive per capsid to the precision of the equivalence principle (η < 10⁻¹⁵, MICROSCOPE). A non-additive term at any scale kills "one quantum per capture."
3. **Flatness.** Ω_k ≠ 0 breaks the top root of §5.
4. **The lag check** (§7, row 4): the per-capsid third-of-a-tick summed over a known mass distribution must give the GR lag; if it gives a different functional form than Φ/c², reading 3 is a picture, not a mechanism.
5. **Scale-dependence of stiffness.** If the only consistent k → G ladder requires a *new* constant per rung, the turtle is a zoo. (§7 row 1 is the computation; a result orders of magnitude off 10⁻³⁸ with no honest rung to absorb it kills §3.)
6. **Ringdown echoes.** A rim that carries the mass is a surface, and a surface reflects: post-merger echoes at the light-crossing delay (Cardoso–Pani). Abedi–Dykaar–Afshordi claimed 2.5σ in GW150914 (2017); the LIGO/Virgo follow-ups are null and bound the reflectivity. A rim that *must* reflect and a bound that falls below its reflectivity kills "hollow" (§6a). A one-way membrane that does not reflect would need saying why, in the gauge, the maximally-behind matter absorbs.
7. **Primordial black holes.** §3a predicts capsid disruption at the horizon only below 2.5 × 10¹³ kg. If PBHs in that mass range are found with a spectrum showing no such edge, §3a's scale is wrong.
8. **No point singularity.** Any observation requiring a true point singularity rather than a Planck-density core (none is known to be possible) would kill §6a's floor.
