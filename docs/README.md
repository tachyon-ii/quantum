## What v2 changed, so the diff is legible

- **Three axioms, not one undifferentiated Ω:** amount (conserved; later named energy), spin (the one property a partless whole can carry), time (a rate presupposes its parameter). Length and mass are downstream *names* (natural units). **Ω is not a singularity** — it has extent, its period, R = c/ω (James's ruling).
- **µ is a plain loop carrying a half-wound phase**, not a Möbius track. Spin ½ is the antiperiodic condition — which is what `mobius_topology/` always tested. Reinstated twice by bees; struck both times.
- **108° is struck everywhere.** The tetrahedral lobe angle is 109.47°; loops are at 90° at every rung; C-12 is a triangle of alphas, O-16 a tetrahedron of alphas.
- **3D is selected by capture stability** (well at 3, shelf at 4, hills above — from the axioms plus arithmetic; Ehrenfest/Tangherlini/Tegmark are prior art), **not** by orthogonality running out and **not** by primes. Both [DEAD].
- **The Coherosphere is retired; the chorus torus replaces it** — a compact 3-manifold with an axis, closed by topology not curvature, no back side. Time is its normal (Milne, given that everyone is comoving — the chorus).
- **c is the capture boundary**, and that is flatness: 2GM/R = c² at the Hubble radius to 1.000 (computed). The first measured number the cosmogony touches. It gives a relation, not invariance; Lorentz invariance is owed.
- **The inversion is by deceleration, not launch speed:** with R = c/ω, Ω spins up for free (ω ∝ m^{−3/2}) and every ejectum leaves at c; capture pulls the early shells back and the late ones overtake. "The first shall be last" is spatial.
- **32.11 restated:** bulk rotation ≈ 0 (measured ~10⁻¹⁰ H₀); Ω's angular momentum lives in local chirality — galaxy-spin handedness is the test.
- **Dark matter stays with the snakeskin** (canon §16). The trailing, terminal and back-side ejecta are not dark matter (gravity at c; shell theorem; the data are local).
- **The paint predicts the sky's spectral shape:** Brownian recoil gives a scale-invariant spectrum (computed, slope −2.0); stroked and resonant paints are falsified; the red tilt demands anti-persistent recoil, Hurst 0.49.
