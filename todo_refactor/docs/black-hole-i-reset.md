## The posit

1. **The core is the nearest model to Ω in this universe:** maximal uniformity, zero distinguishability — an **i-annihilator**. i is a vector; at the core the vector ceases to exist.
2. **Hawking leakage is NOT the old i escaping.** It is **new i on reset substrate** — RAM after the three-finger salute; `git reset --hard`. The chipper recycles substrate, not contents.
3. **Mechanism:** quantum tunneling at the horizon ("blue LED style"). Prior art: **Parikh–Wilczek 2000** — Hawking flux derived as tunneling across the horizon; respectable, not fringe. The corpus's addition: *what* tunnels is i.
4. **The photon re-vectoring:** a photon (c-bound carrier) reaching the horizon has all future directions pointing inward — the carrier is committed. At the horizon **i is peeled off the carrier and re-vectors a new outgoing mode.** Maps onto the standard pair-creation picture (partner in, new quantum born at the horizon pointing out) with the causal role reassigned: the outgoing vector is set by i at the surface, not by the carrier's history — hence thermal blankness.
