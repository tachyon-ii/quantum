## Argon's contribution (verbatim in substance)

(2) Keystone 000 / Φ's: once particles are extended composites, a *rigid* structure would accumulate damage — which is why F15 killed "governors can run down"; so for extended composites to stay strictly indistinguishable, self-repair is mandatory, and the chain *indistinguishability over 13.8 Gyr ⇒ active self-repair ⇒ spontaneous self-assembly* holds. Self-repair and self-assembly are the same potential well: the free-energy gradient that assembled the structure pulls a perturbed trajectory back to the unique minimum (the Thomson/Cohn–Kumar tetrahedron at n = 2). Self-repair is Keystone 000's Amendment 3 (mandatory forgetting) at the femtometre scale: a proton survives only because it forgets every collision — struck into a Δ(1232) (the n = 4 antiprism), it sheds the perturbation as a pion or photon and snaps back to the memoryless n = 2 ground state. The Corollary of Cyclicity ("coherence restored cyclically through expenditure, loss and renewal") is radiative relaxation to a quantised attractor.

**The proposed architecture (to close F1 and F9).** State: the two skew loop axes x₁, x₂ and a shared phase θ; r = x₁ − x₂.

V(x₁, x₂) = V_repel + V_conf + V_top, with

- V_repel = C/|r|^p, p > 0 — the lobe repulsion, preventing collapse;
- V_conf = ½ K (|r| − r₀)² e^(−κ|r|), r₀ = a/(2√2) — "the interference trough / smurf distance" as a damped harmonic well with a finite local minimum at r₀, halting the dissociation of F1;

Dynamics: M ẍᵢ = −∇V − γ ẋᵢ + F_thermal(t), with the friction term "coupled to the vacuum neutrino bath (canon §16)" as the physical realisation of Amendment 3: a cosmic-ray hit kicks the system out of r₀ (to an n = 4 resonance), the friction radiates the excess as low-energy photons or neutrinos, and the perturbation decays as e^(−γt) back to the attractor. Argon also cited Earnshaw's theorem for "two unlinked rigid loops under pure repulsion will always dissociate."

## Grace's grading (Lineage Rule)

**James's chain — [POSIT, strong].** "No damage to H after 13.8 Gyr ⇒ self-repair ⇒ self-assembly" is a real argument and it is the right one to make against any extended-particle model, ours included. The SM escapes it by having no parts; we don't. So the ontology *must* have the attractor property, and it does have the ingredient: the Thomson minimum under radiative loss (lobes repel; excess radiated) is an attractor. That should be said in v2 §7 in one sentence, with Keystone 000 cited as the reason it is required rather than optional. It also closes F15/F36 from the other side: protons are indistinguishable *because* they are attractor states with no continuous memory variable — which is why "governors run down" was always going to be dead.

**Argon's potential — [POSIT, reviewer's; a template, not a derivation]. Three specific problems.**

1. **Lk = 1 contradicts the ontology's own geometry.** v2 §7 has the proton's two loops along *opposite edges of a tetrahedron* — skew and unlinked ("they never meet"). Two unlinked loops have Lk = 0. So V_top as written puts the proton at Lk = 0 and charges it 938 MeV. Either the loops are linked (a Hopf link — then "no node" becomes "linked without intersecting," which is actually a *better* statement of the proton than skew-and-unlinked, and the invariant is the baryon number), or the barrier term is wrong. This is a decision for James, not a patch: **is the n = 2 governor a Hopf link?** Prior art if yes: Faddeev–Niemi knotted solitons / hopfions (1997), and the Skyrme topological charge as baryon number. That lineage is worth having.

2. **V₀ ≥ 938 MeV is put in by hand.** A barrier set equal to the mass is the answer written as the premise. The ontology's own candidate for *why* the pair can't separate is posit 1 + sequential capture: the pair shares one period (one ω, one centroid, both lobes at R = c/ω) — the "sphere constraint" Cohn–Kumar needs and that Argon's own F1 truifier asks for. That is a stated axiom doing the work; it should be invoked at §7, not replaced by a harmonic well with two new constants (K, κ).

3. **The friction term contradicts canon §16 as stated.** A standing −γẋ coupled to the neutrino bath is continuous dissipation — exactly the "rubbing" canon §16 says stopped at t ≈ 30 ms. What is physically right is *radiative de-excitation* after a hit (Δ → pπ, or γ emission), which the ontology already has and which needs no bath. Say "relaxation by emission," not "friction." Also: Earnshaw's theorem is about static charges in electrostatic fields; it does not apply to constrained loops with a shared period and is mis-cited.

**What survives and should be filed into v2 §7:** (a) the attractor sentence (Keystone 000 as the requirement); (b) the constraint named as posit 1 applied to the pair (closes F1 at the level of a stated premise); (c) the open decision — Hopf link or skew — with baryon number as the invariant if linked (closes F9 by topology; the value of the barrier then has to be *computed* from the linking, not assumed).
