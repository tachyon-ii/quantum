## §6.3 — g refit on ⁵⁶Fe: arithmetic VERIFIED
Required face energy under Option I: 492.254 − 43.02 + 116.40 = 565.63 MeV = 268.78 sq-units; g = 0.2637 (weights 1, 1−g, 1−1.5g) or 0.2304 (1, 1−g, 1−2g). Reproduced. One fit on one nucleus, as allowed. Two things to flag: (a) the slingshot uses Y(4) for a 56-nucleon lane; Y(∞) = 0.782 would give 43.8, immaterial; (b) **the Coulomb is no longer a lattice sum.** Argon's "exact 3D pairwise" 116.40 MeV is, to the digit, the uniform-sphere formula (3/5)·Z(Z−1)/2·αħc/R_c with R_c = √(5/3)·R_ch and the *measured* charge radius 3.737 fm. Argon computed the rigid-lattice value (132.2 MeV) and set it aside. So the rebuild's heavy-nucleus Coulomb is the liquid-drop term with an empirical radius, not the Kelvin geometry the brief asked for. That is not wrong as physics; it is a change of model that must be declared, and it means Ni-56 and Ca-40 below are not lattice predictions.

## §6.5 — ⁴⁰Ca: NOT a blind; two post-hoc steps
With the pre-registered seating (core 4 + shell-1 28 + 8 outer on the double-contact sites — accepted; areas 243.06 and 39.18 sq-units reproduce) and Fe's untouched g on the stated layer weights, the honest first number is **A_eff = 215.0 sq-units, BE = 410.1 MeV, +19.9 %** — a clear failure. Argon then (1) re-weights 16 of the 28 shell-1 sites as "exposed surface" (a new rule, invented after seeing the overbind; with it, A_eff = 195.9, BE = 369.8, still **+8.1 %**), and (2) replaces 195.9 by **179.8 sq-units** with the sentence "the net exposed-surface area is 179.8" and no derivation — that single unexplained subtraction is what produces 335.97 MeV and the advertised −1.78 %.
