## 1. The one thing three readers found without knowing it — 0.34 fm

Three numbers from three unrelated places land on the same length:

| source | number | where |
|---|---|---|
| neutron mean-square charge radius, PDG | ⟨r²⟩_E = −0.1161(22) fm² → if the e⁺ is central and the e⁻ rides a lane at r_e, **r_e = √0.1161 = 0.341 fm** | `tests.md` L377–380 (the benchmark); v2 §7b's "positive core, negative skin" |

Three independent 0.34s at the 1 % level. **Grade: [COINCIDENCE until pre-registered].** Two things must be decided *before* anyone looks again: (i) which Kelvin-cell length canon identifies with 0.841 fm (centre-to-hex gives a/2 = 0.343; centre-to-square gives 0.971; circumradius 1.086 — the choice is currently free, which is how numerology gets in); (ii) whether canon's neutron has the e⁻ *on an outer lane* (v2 §7b) or *at a vertex opposite the e⁺* (canon §11: "e⁺ and e⁻ sit at opposite vertices of the octahedron"). Those two canon statements disagree, and the sign of ⟨r²⟩_n discriminates: opposite vertices at equal radius gives ⟨r²⟩ = +e r² − e r² = **0**; central e⁺ with outer e⁻ gives **−e r_e²**. The measurement is negative. So canon §11's wording is the one that has to move, and v2 §7b's is the one the data pick. [One afternoon; first real test of where the carriers sit.]

**The caveat that keeps it honest.** The Foldy term — the relativistic smearing tied to the neutron's magnetic moment — is 3κ_n(ħc)²/2m² = **−0.127 fm²**, almost the whole measured −0.116. In Dirac theory the neutron's "intrinsic" charge distribution is nearly neutral. A geometric model that gets −0.116 from a charge *distribution* must therefore either show that its circulating carrier produces the Foldy contribution *as the same effect* (plausible — the moment and the skin would both be the e⁻ lane) or it is double-counting. That has to be written down before the 0.34 is claimed.

## 3. Corrections canon owes to itself (found by reading its own tests)

- **HFQPO discriminator 5 is wrong as stated.** `hfqpo_phi_pmode.md` L21–22 claims "3:2 preserved under jitter"; its own L36 gives the ladder ω_n² = κ_r² + (nπc_s/L)². A 3:2 pair needs κ_r² = 1.4a². Then d ln f/d ln L is 0.417 for n = 1 and 0.741 for n = 2 — **the ratio drifts by 0.32 per unit fractional change in L**; varying κ_r drifts it the other way. Only a *common* rescaling (g_GR or M) preserves the ratio, and with κ_r = 0 the ladder gives 2:1, not 3:2. So "jitter with fixed ratio" is not a prediction of the cavity ladder — if anything it is evidence against one. Canon §35 lists it as a live discriminator; amend. [COMPUTED — ten lines of algebra.]
- **The EHT chromaticity falsifier is degenerate with plasma until a protocol exists.** `deprecated/black-holes-v3.md` L183 — the archive's own last word on the metric — says Φ-saturation modifies the shadow *achromatically*; the 5–10 % chromatic shift canon §35 calls "the cheapest big falsifier" must then come from the boundary transmissivity T(ω,Φ), which is an emissivity/absorption effect, exactly what plasma also produces. The number is not even stable in the archive (5 % at `data_sources.md` L66, 5–10 % at L9/L81). It is a falsifier only with a written separation protocol: ≥3 bands, polarisation, decorrelation from time variability. Half a day to write; until then, regrade from "falsifier" to "signature."
- **The g = 2 choke has a second, independent failure.** `g_minus_2_grid.csv` (14,641 points) and `g_vs_eps_geom.csv`: every natural correction in the toolkit — time-dilated current, relativistic inertia, finite width g = 2(1 − kε^p) — pushes g **below** 2, the wrong sign for the Schwinger anomaly (2.00232). The archive's rule would make the proton a boson. ## 4. Mathematics that supports v2 §18 (the twist is a phase)

- **The double-cover identity** — `one-reality-two-expressions-v2.md` L148–150: E_Möbius(L, n) = ½[E_per(L, n) + E_per(L, n+1)] = **E_per(2L, 2n+1)**, i.e. the half-wound phase on a loop of length L is exactly the odd sector of a plain periodic loop of length 2L; k_n = (2n+1)π/L. One line that says what the antiperiodic condition *buys* with no strip anywhere. Write it into v2 §18.
- **A real Möbius band carries both ladders** — `annihilation.md` L256–285: separating variables on a Möbius band, even transverse modes are periodic along the loop and odd transverse modes are antiperiodic. So the strip's geometry does *not* force spin-½; it offers both and the half-wound phase must be imposed as a boundary condition anyway. That is the reason the strip was retired, stated as mathematics. Cite in v2 §18.
- **The supply-chain argument** — `mobius-v3.1.md` L72: "no conceivable supply chain could provide pre-assembled Möbius pairs instantly, anywhere, whenever an electron changes shells." A clean, reusable kill for any composite-photon model; worth keeping as the one-line reason photons are ejecta/wake in canon, not assemblies.
- **R_eff = ħ/(2mc)** — `one-reality` L138, L189: the loop radius a particle of mass m needs. For a 0.05 eV neutrino that is **≈ 2 µm** — macroscopic. Canon's neutrino-bath friction (§16) uses a neutrino *size*; it should own this number or refute it. For the electron, 1.9×10⁻¹³ m, half the reduced Compton length, relevant to the crystalline electron's core.

## 7. The contradictions — why the corpus is not one delusion

The readers found the files disagreeing with each other on: *what a photon is* (same object as the neutrino / a bound ν–ν̄ pair / explicitly not Möbius but a wake — `mobius-v3.1` L72 kills the first two); *what an electron is* (diamond lattice of like-matter pairs / mixed Majorana pair / inside a folded quark — `tests.md` L329 declares the first falsified); *what a neutron is* (p + e⁻ + ν̄ stabiliser / Sierpinski shell / ν̄ spinner in a hexaflake); *the nucleon polyhedron* (Sierpinski tetrahedron → truncated tetrahedron → truncated octahedron; only the last is canon); *He-4 topology* (ring of 4 with 4 bonds vs tetrahedron with 6, in the same document); *genesis* (T-bone vs first wobble; polymer detonation removed in one file and kept in two others); *Majorana* (three files no, canon yes); *chirality scope* (global from the first particle vs canon's local); *the stability peak* (He-4 vs the alpha ladder's rising BE/A); *dark-matter mass* (2–15 MeV / m_p / PBHs); *black-hole information* (no destruction vs maximum-entropy processors vs canon's annihilators); *ring chromaticity* (achromatic metric vs 5–10 % shift). Several LLMs, several answers, no convergence — which is exactly what makes the few convergences (0.34 fm; the double-cover identity; the strip carrying both parities) worth more than they would be from one voice.
