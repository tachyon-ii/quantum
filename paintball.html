Paintball Sphere

A spinning, precessing ejector paints an expanding sphere one pulse at a time. A step that shares a factor with 360 resonates: ten teeth and bare sky. A coprime step paints every direction once before any twice. Jitter on the step turns the comb into a cloud.

Buckets are 1° × 1° on the surface, 64,800 of them. Colour is count per bucket on a log scale, cold to hot. The sphere grows by a fixed fraction every turn — Hubble-like — and the camera backs off to keep it in frame. The spin axis walks the full sphere of directions on a Fibonacci spiral — pole to pole over 360 turns, swinging round by the precession angle each turn (the golden angle by default, which never closes). Counts are shown per solid angle. The recoil kick instead random-walks the axis. Every pulse leaves the centre.
