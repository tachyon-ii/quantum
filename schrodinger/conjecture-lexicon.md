## B. Defined here — the *cosmological / ontological* layer (ours)

BP&R stop at one particle. This layer wraps their localized mechanics in an origin and a universal loop.

- **Ω (the Origin)** — the pre-fracture baseline. Infinitely uniform, infinitely dense, **dimensionless**: no distance (m), no mass (kg), no time (s). Equivalently — and this is load-bearing — **Ω contains zero information**: nothing distinguishable from anything else, no here/there, no before/after. Status: **[AXIOM, chosen]** (per capsid-audit Part 13 — Ω is posited, not derived; it establishes the origin is *simple*, not that it is *unique*). *Key identity:* Ω (zero information, no-i) and the heat death (maximal entropy, all distinctions erased, no-i) are the **same state**; the universe is the parenthesis of information between them.

- **μ (the Primitive)** — the minimal persistent spherical rotator (a BP&R object at its floor); the singular seed that precipitates from Ω and underlies the entire particle zoo. **What μ *is* beyond "minimal persistent rotation" is TBA** (James): whether literally a Möbius loop or something yet more primitive is open. Adopt BP&R's "spherical rotation" as the name for *what μ does*.

- **i (Information / Difference)** — the operator that injects **difference** into Ω: the possibility that one thing is not another (distinguishability = one bit). It is the first asymmetry; geometrically, the twist that makes twist-2 *distinct from* twist-1. It drives the `goto NEXT` loop. Note the deep pun that is also a claim: **i = information** (Shannon/Hartley) and **i = the quaternion rotation generator** (BP&R) are asserted to be the same object (§C). *Boundary conditions:* Ω has no i (nothing to distinguish); heat death loses i (nothing left distinguishable). i exists only in the middle.
  **Amendment (2026-08-26, per `black-hole-i-reset.md`): token-i vs type-i.** *Token-i* = a particular instantiated distinction (a vector) — **destructible** (annihilated at black-hole cores, the local Ω-points; the Second Law wins the body). *Type-i* = the pattern/capacity for distinction — **persists** (new tokens minted at horizons; the liturgy's `i > /dev/null → FALSE` is a claim about type-i, not token-i). Also policed here: i is **not a traveler** — c bounds metric propagation; i is pre-metric, so "escapes the horizon" means "was never in c's jurisdiction," never "moves faster than c" (the C-as-speed error, standing flag).

- **C (the first ordering) — CORRECTED from Gordon's "speed of information."** ⚠️ **C cannot be a *speed* at or near Ω, by James's own Axiom #2:** a speed has units of m/s, and at Ω there is no metre and no second. "Faster than c" is therefore not merely speculative — it is *dimensionally undefined before length and time exist.* So C is **not a velocity**. C is redefined here as **the ordering primitive of the first differences** — the (pre-metric, unit-free) sequence in which SU(2) relationships settle *before* a metric exists to measure a rate. Once geometry congeals and a metre/second exist, *then* a genuine relation-propagation speed appears and relaxes toward c (the railway-station observation: we measure the mature, stationary value and call it constant). Until then, "speed of information" is a category error the lexicon must refuse.

## D. First derived result in the shared language: what one μ generates

Gordon's question: does the spherical rotation of a single μ generate mass *and* length, or does that require the intersection of two twists? The honest answer is a **clean split**, and it falls straight out of BP&R + the ring-crossing sim (audit Part 1):

- **Mass — from a SINGLE μ.** BP&R: m = ℏω/c². One rotator, one frequency, one mass. No second twist required. ✓
- **(Proper) time — from a SINGLE μ.** ω is ticks-per-time; the rotation *is* the first clock; a lone μ carries its own proper-time tick. ✓ (Coordinate time — a *shared* time — is relational and needs ≥2; see below.)
- **Length — STRICTLY requires two twists.** A single rotator has no intrinsic length (BP&R: the core contracts to a point). **Distance is definitionally relational** — a "here" distinguished from a "there" — and one μ has no "there." Length is therefore born only at the **first crossing** (μ ⊗ μ), which is exactly what the ring-crossing sim shows: N=1 has no lobes, no internal distance; **N=2 gives the first 4-lobed extended shape** — the first object with distances between its parts. And the relation only *is* a relation (rather than two isolated μ) because there is **difference — `i` — between the twists.** So: **`i` is the operator that turns two μ into μ-related-to-μ, i.e. into the first length.**

**Summary rule (adopt into the lexicon):** `μ → {mass, proper-time}`; `μ ⊗_i μ → {length, coordinate-time}`. Mass and the clock are intrinsic to one twist; **space is relational and needs two twists made distinct by i.** This is the novel's "small compared with what?" made rigorous — and it means the fracture Ω → {μ + i} is *necessary* for a spatial universe, not incidental: without i there is a twist but no *second, distinguishable* twist, hence no distance, hence no geometry. Length is the first thing i buys.

If so, length may be intrinsic to a single μ's full traversal after all — a genuine open fork worth computing).*
