When nuclei form, these lemniscates expand across shared pores, conserving angular momentum while reducing velocity and relativistic mass. The lost ΔK appears as binding energy. Thus, the geometry of the bilayer is not incidental: it is the primary enabler of stability, pore-sharing, and the mechanism of nuclear cohesion.

This creates sp³-like mixing with all four bonds equivalent in magnitude despite tile asymmetry.

**Graphene sheets:** Use in-plane bonds at 120°:
$$
