## Quark Theory v8: The Hexaflake Graphene Model

This document retires all previous versions. It presents a unified theory where matter exists in two primary conformations derived from a single, fundamental building block: a tetrahedral `n=2` neutrino pair.

---

### 1. The Bedrock of Reality: The Möbius Loop

The universe is built from a single elementary particle: the **`n=1` neutrino**, a Möbius loop that has captured energy to create mass. The most common and stable form this particle takes is the **`n=2` neutrino pair**.

### 2. The Fundamental Building Block: The `n=2` Tetrahedral Unit

The `n=2` neutrino pair is the "Lego brick" of all matter. Its properties are defined by its geometry:

* **Structure:** It forms a **tetrahedral** structure, analogous to a carbon atom.
* **Fields:** It possesses two pairs of North-South magnetic poles at its vertices and two pairs of positive-negative electric fields at its centroid.
* **Perfect Null Hypothesis:** In its ground state, these fields are perfectly balanced and externally weak. However, when induced by an external field, they become self-reinforcing.
* **Chirality (Matter vs. Antimatter):** The two chiral forms (left-handed and right-handed) of this `n=2` tetrahedral unit are what we define as **matter** and **antimatter**.

### 3. The Two Conformations of Matter

This single, chiral, tetrahedral building block can assemble into two distinct, stable conformations, which explains the difference between free leptons and bound nuclear matter.

#### The Crystalline Form: Diamond (Leptons)

When `n=2` units of a **single chirality** assemble, they form a pure, diamond-like crystal. This is the structure of free leptons:
* An **electron (e-)** is a diamond-like crystal made entirely of *matter* `n=2` units.
* A **positron (e+)** is a diamond-like crystal made entirely of *antimatter* `n=2` units.

This single-chirality structure is exceptionally stable but relatively inert, explaining the nature of free electrons and positrons.

#### The Sheet Form: Hexaflake Graphene (Bound Matter)

When `n=2` units of **both chiralities** assemble, they form a two-dimensional sheet analogous to graphene. The most stable and efficient geometry for this is the **Hexaflake Fractal**, which has a **perfect 50:50 split** of matter and antimatter components.

![Hexaflake Fractal](images/hexaflake-fractal.png)

This hexaflake architecture is the universe's elegant solution to building stable, composite matter:

* **Perfect Separation:** As the colored hexaflake demonstrates, the geometry provides a perfect, self-assembling separation mechanism. Each matter (green) hexagon is insulated from other matter hexagons by a layer of antimatter (black) hexagons, and vice-versa. This creates a stable, charge-neutral, and non-annihilating 2D "graphene" of nuclear matter.
* **The Central Pore:** The hole in the center is a critical feature, providing the natural channel for the lemniscate bonding currents that hold 3D nucleons together.

The 3D structure of nucleons (protons and neutrons) is then a result of the stable folding or stacking of these fundamental 2D hexaflake sheets.

### 4. Nucleon Capsids: Self-Assembly

The three-dimensional structure of a nucleon (a proton or neutron) is not a fundamental object but an emergent one, created by the self-assembly of 2D hexaflake sheets.

We posit that a stable nucleon capsid is an assembly of **8 hexaflake sheets**. This number is not arbitrary; it is the minimum required to fold into a stable, quasi-spherical, 3D structure that satisfies the geometric and bonding requirements. The resulting shape is a **Truncated Octahedron**, which features:
* **8 Hexagonal Ports:** Formed by the central pores of the 8 constituent hexaflake sheets.
* **6 Square Ports:** These are emergent "full-defect" structures formed at the intersections where the edges of the folding hexaflakes meet.

This process is analogous to protein folding. A linear chain of amino acids (our 2D sheets) folds into a complex, functional 3D shape, guided by the fundamental interactions of its components.

### 5. The Nucleon Tokamak: Emergent Charge and Twisted Paths

The internal dynamics of the nucleon—the "tokamak"—are an emergent property of the assembled capsid's geometry and the fields of its constituent `n=2` units.

#### Emergent Charge

The positive and negative charges of the electron and positron are not fundamental properties in this model. They are an emergent consequence of field orientation. In simple terms: **"if more of the +ve fields of the constituent n=2 units point inward, you end up with a naked, unbalanced -ve field on the outside."** The diametrically opposite properties of electrons and positrons are a direct result of the opposite chirality of their fundamental `n=2` building blocks, which causes their fields to orient in mirror-image configurations.

#### Twisted Toroidal Paths

Within the nucleon, the 8 hexaflake faces create a complex interference pattern for the internal fields. This doesn't create simple, concentric "shells" for the `e-` and `e+` currents to flow in. Instead, the interference of the fields from the 8 faces forces the charge paths into a series of **twisted, interlocking toroids**—the tokamak.

This toroidal geometry is the key to stability. It is the structure that allows the matter (`e-`) and antimatter (`e+`) components of the neutron to co-exist in a dynamic, phase-separated dance without annihilating.
