# **CMB Event–Peak Sequence v4**

## **Preamble: The Universe's First Photograph**

The Cosmic Microwave Background is the universe's first photograph, a fossilized echo of the moment of creation. It is not a random pattern of noise, but a detailed historical record, the grandest archeological artifact in existence.

This document provides a complete, step-by-step narrative that deciphers this record. It maps each of the observed acoustic peaks in the CMB's power spectrum to a specific, foundational event in the early universe—from the first congealing of matter to the ignition of the first stars.

By framing the sequence as an alternating rhythm of **Events (W)** and their resulting **Peaks (P)**, we demonstrate how the grandest cosmological signal is the direct and necessary consequence of the geometric and resonant principles that govern the smallest particles. This is the story of the universe's birth, written in ancient light.

* **Outcome:** 
  
  * At the edges, annihilation events ($\nu + \bar\nu \to E$, etc.) provide heat and HPHT (high-pressure/high-temperature) conditions that sustain growth.

### **P1 – The First Peak (annihilation-driven cascade)**

* **Nature:** This is the peak of the annihilation cascade. The rise and fall of this first, largest peak maps the moment when the rate of energy released by annihilations at the halo edges (see W1) reached its maximum intensity, before cosmic expansion began to overwhelm the process, cooling the reactions and damping the signal. It is the echo of the universe's first great wave of creative destruction.
* **Signature:** This is the largest CMB peak, capturing the first major **growth–damping** cycle driven by halo-edge annihilations (W1 growth) and cosmic expansion (damping).

* **Consequence:** **P2** is smaller than P1 (expansion + reduced coherence) and consistent with an **on→off** medium effect: the damping envelope reverts toward baseline while $\theta_s$ remains set by geometry and co-tuned backgrounds.

* **Trigger:** Local halo accretion runs out of like-matter. Protection fails.

* **Chain of annihilations:**

    $$

## The Setting of $c$ – what / when / where / how / why

### Premises

1. **No singularity:** the "Big Blob" was **not** a pointlike singularity (zero degrees of variance); it had finite extent and structure.
2. **It oscillated:** the Blob's essential property was **oscillation**—a resonant field, not a static lump.

---

### What is $c$?

* $c$ is the **invariant signal speed in vacuum** (the lightlike slope of the causal cone).

* In field terms, for the master wave $R$,
  
  $$
  \mathcal L=\tfrac12\,\chi\,(\partial_t R)^2-\tfrac12\,K\,(\nabla R)^2
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\omega^2=\frac{K}{\chi}\,k^2,\ \ \boxed{c=\sqrt{K/\chi}}.
  $$
  
  Here $K$ is the substrate's **spatial stiffness** (how strongly it resists curvature) and $\chi$ its **temporal susceptibility** (how much energy sits in time variation).

---

### When was $c$ set?

* **During W0 → W1 (the Great Congealing).**
  As the Blob cools/expands, the background ("silent substrate") **enters its vacuum phase**. In that transition, the **ratio** $K/\chi$ settles to a universal value. From that moment onward, **vacuum $c$ is fixed**.

---

### Where does this apply?

* **Outside defects and media**: the bulk substrate away from bound-energy defects (mass) and away from any polarizable medium.
* Inside media (plasma, halos) light is **not** a pure vacuum mode; it mixes with matter and propagates as a **photon–matter polariton**.

---

### How?

* Think string-wave analogy: $v=\sqrt{T/\mu}$. If both tension $T$ and linear density $\mu$ scale with the same bulk energy, their **ratio** (hence $v$) is **independent of total $E$ or size $R$** once the phase is fixed.
* Likewise, in the vacuum phase both $K$ and $\chi$ inherit the same global scaling from the Blob; their **ratio** $K/\chi$ becomes a **phase constant**, giving a universal $c$.
* This avoids "$c$ set by total $E$" pitfalls (unit/scale dependence) while tightly following the energy into the **two moduli that matter**.

---

### Why is "$c$ in a medium" different?

* The EM field **induces polarization currents** in matter. The propagating eigenmode becomes a **coupled photon–matter mode** with dispersion
  
  $$
  \omega^2=c^2k^2+\mathrm{Re}\,\Pi(\omega,k),
  $$
  
  equivalently $n^2(\omega)=1+\chi_{\text{medium}}(\omega)$ and $v_{\text{phase}}=c/n(\omega)$.

* **Vacuum $c$ does not change**; the **mode** changes in a medium. Front velocity in vacuum remains $c$. **Cherenkov** is $v>c/n$ (in-medium), not $>c$ (vacuum).

---

### Fit to the W/P timeline

* **W0 → W1:** vacuum phase appears; **$c=\sqrt{K/\chi}$** is set.
* **W1 → P1:** the universe is a **medium** (plasma + halo polarization). Recombination microphysics and diffusion reflect **in-medium** dispersion/opacity, not a change of vacuum $c$.
* **W2 (reset):** quench/freeze switches off the in-medium tweak for CMB-relevant bands; propagation reverts toward vacuum behaviour.
* **Later (P3, stars/novae):** localized media again modify modes (small, scale-specific), still leaving vacuum $c$ intact.

---

### Consequences & non-consequences

* **Not dependent on Blob size $R$ or total energy $E$:** unless you break the common scaling of $K$ and $\chi$, $c$ is **ratio-set**, not $E$- or $R$-set.
* **No singularity needed:** a finite, oscillatory Blob has the degrees of variance required to yield finite $K,\chi$ and a finite $c$.
* **Observables:** small high-$\ell$ CMB damping tweaks (in-medium), CRR signatures, Cherenkov in dense media—all compatible with a **fixed vacuum $c$**.

---

### One-line takeaway

> $c$ was set when the Blob's energy partitioned (at W1) into a vacuum with fixed spatial stiffness $K$ and temporal susceptibility $\chi$; their ratio $K/\chi$ defines the universal vacuum speed. Later "changes of speed" are medium effects—changes of the propagating eigenmode—not changes of the vacuum constant.
> 
> *"Who" set it remains delightfully mysterious.*

## The Emergence of Space and Time (pre-math → math)

**Premath (W0):** A finite, oscillatory "Big Blob" with degrees of variance but **no metric** yet. Only topological/logical statements are meaningful (e.g., "there exists a resonant substrate," "no singularity").

**Birth of the metric (W1):** The background cools into a **vacuum phase** with two constitutive moduli:

$$
\mathcal L=\tfrac12\,\chi\,(\partial_t R)^2-\tfrac12\,K\,(\nabla R)^2,
\qquad \omega^2=\frac{K}{\chi}\,k^2,\quad \boxed{c=\sqrt{K/\chi}}.
$$

* $K$: spatial stiffness (response to curvature).
* $\chi$: temporal susceptibility (energy in time variation).
  At W1 their **ratio** fixes the invariant causal speed $c$. Only **after** this lock-in can we speak coherently of time $t$, distance $x$, and write field equations.

**How math becomes legal:** With a hyperbolic PDE and invariant $c$,

* a metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ exists (locally Minkowski; FRW after coarse-graining),
* Noether's theorems give energy–momentum from symmetries,
* clocks and rulers appear operationally: periodic modes (e.g., neutrino/photon oscillations) set **time**, correlation length sets **distance**.

**Medium vs vacuum (W1→P1):** The universe is an ionized **medium** (plasma + halo polarization). Light propagates as a **photon–matter polariton** with dispersion $\omega^2=c^2k^2+\mathrm{Re}\,\Pi(\omega,k)$, so phase/group speeds differ from $c$ (index $n(\omega)\neq1$). **Vacuum $c$ does not change**—the **mode** does.

**Reset (W2) and memory:** The radiative quench freezes leptons and switches off the in-medium tweak for CMB-relevant bands. Propagation tends back toward vacuum behaviour, leaving the **CMB's acoustic and damping structure** as a fossil record of W1→W2 dynamics.

**Why the "singularity" is unnecessary:** A pointlike singularity has **zero degrees of variance**; no oscillation, no moduli, no $c$. A finite, oscillatory Blob lets $K,\chi$ be finite, hence $c=\sqrt{K/\chi}$ finite, and math can begin.

**Five W-questions, answered:**

* **What** was set? The **ratio** $K/\chi$ that defines vacuum $c$.
* **When?** During **W1** Great Congealing.
* **Where?** In the **vacuum** regions away from defects/media.
* **How?** Phase transition of the substrate to a hyperbolic, Lorentz-invariant regime.
* **Why?** Stable wave propagation requires a fixed causal cone; the vacuum phase provides it (universality of the ratio).
* **"Who?"** remains poetically open.

## **Physical Model: Lepton Accretion & Protection**

### **1. First $\bar\nu$ (antineutrino) seed.**

A local antineutrino $\bar\nu$ forms an $\overline{\mathcal N}_2$ tetrahedral motif, the first accretion kernel.
Mirror case: a local neutrino $\nu$ seed and $\mathcal N_2$ accretion kernel for the electron branch.

---

### **2. Autocatalysis.**

* The seed draws in more like neutrinos/antineutrinos, building a halo:
  
  $$
  \bar\nu \;\to\; \Sigma \bar\nu, 
\qquad 
\nu \;\to\; \Sigma \nu
  $$

> **Notation.** $\Sigma\nu$ / $\Sigma\bar\nu$: like-species neutrino/antineutrino halos; $\langle e^\pm\rangle$: accreting proto-lepton lattices; $e^\pm$: frozen, final leptons.

* Meanwhile, the vicinity still contains the opposite species ($\nu$ or $\bar\nu$) and opposite proto-accretors $\langle e^\mp \rangle$.
* Autocatalysis: the bigger the halo, the faster it captures more of its own kind.

---

### **3. Halo-fed accretion → freeze at W2.**

* Positron channel (antimatter):
  
  $$
  \Sigma \bar\nu \;+\; \langle e^+ \rangle \;+\; \Sigma \bar\nu 
\;\;\xRightarrow[\;W2\;]{\text{quench}}\;\; e^+
  $$

* Electron channel (matter):
  
  $$
  \Sigma \nu \;+\; \langle e^- \rangle \;+\; \Sigma \nu 
\;\;\xRightarrow[\;W2\;]{\text{quench}}\;\; e^-
  $$

At the W2 boundary event, a radiative front anneals the proto-lattice and freezes it into the final, universal lepton size.

---

### **4. Single-soldier protection (local).**

Invaders of the opposite matter species are neutralised one-by-one at the halo edge:

$$
\nu \;+\; \bar\nu \;\;\to\;\; E \quad\text{(harmless radiation)}
$$

---

### **5. Large-scale protection (edge).**

Invading opposite matter proto-accretors are intercepted by the halo perimeter defence in depth:

$$
\Sigma \bar\nu \;+\; \langle e^- \rangle \;\;\to\;\; E,
\qquad
\Sigma \nu \;+\; \langle e^+ \rangle \;\;\to\;\; E
$$

These annihilation events both protect the core and inject energy to sustain HPHT (high-pressure/high-temperature) crystallisation.

---

### **6. Vulnerability by halo depletion.**

If the halo is exhausted (local $\Sigma \nu$ or $\Sigma \bar\nu$ drops below threshold), protection fails:

$$
\langle e^+ \rangle \;+\; \{\Sigma \nu \;\text{or}\; \langle e^- \rangle\} \;\;\to\;\; E,
$$

$$
\langle e^- \rangle \;+\; \{\Sigma \bar\nu \;\text{or}\; \langle e^+ \rangle\} \;\;\to\;\; E
$$

Unprotected proto-accretors are annihilated before they reach the big freeze at W2.
