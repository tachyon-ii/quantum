## 4. The Second & Third Generations: Muons and Taus (Excitations)

Muons and taus are **not new building blocks**; they are **excited states of the electron’s diamond lattice** that appear **after** W2.

* **Muon ($\mu^{-}$): Allotrope (stacking fault)**
  Analogy: hexagonal diamond (**lonsdaleite**)—same boundary, denser packing energy → higher mass; relaxes back to $e^{-}$ + neutrinos.

* **Tau ($\tau^{-}$): High-frequency vibrational mode**
  Analogy: the diamond lattice “ringing” at extreme frequency; ultra-short lifetime; often decays via $\mu$ to $e$.

**Why same charge & $g\simeq 2$?** Charge and the near-Dirac $g$ live on the **Möbius boundary condition**, shared by all modes. Changing internal packing changes **mass**, not gauge coupling.

## 6. Selection Rules (CC / NC & Oscillations)

* **Charged current (CC):** a neutrino’s **flavor kernel** projects onto the **matching lattice mode** at the interaction point (energy thresholds permitting):
  
  $$
  \nu_{e}\!\to e,\quad \nu_{\mu}\!\to \mu,\quad \nu_{\tau}\!\to \tau .
  $$
  
  Thresholds reflect the extra packing energy required for $\mu$ and especially $\tau$ modes.

* **Neutral current (NC):** flavor-blind **capsid excitation/spallation** without lepton mode promotion; the neutrino exits with altered energy/direction and hadrons are produced.

* **Oscillations:** the flavor kernel **precesses** in flight; dense media **tilt** the eigenbasis (MSW-like). Thus a source $\nu_e$ may arrive and interact as $\nu_\mu$ or $\nu_\tau$ — the mode selected **at interaction** matches the kernel at that point.
